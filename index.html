I am Assistant Professor of Marketing and Asness Faculty Fellow at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business.

I study the consequences of economic frictions — bounded rationality, informational, and otherwise — across various settings, and their effect on marketing actions and firms. Recent work examines left-digit bias and its market consequences, categorical thinking over continuous attributes, and loss-leader pricing in retail gasoline.

I received my Ph.D. in Quantitative Marketing from the University of Texas at Austin in 2023.
